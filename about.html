About mingle

We want to change the way people discover and connect with each other.

The purpose behind Mingle

Our vision is to bring excitement and curiosity back into online dating. We want people to discover potential connections without relying on perfectly curated photographs.

You know what you're looking for, but not exactly who you'll find. At Mingle, you can explore potential matches based on your own preferences. Let curiosity do the rest.
